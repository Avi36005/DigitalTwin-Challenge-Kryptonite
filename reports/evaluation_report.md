### Selection disclosures

* Eligibility was restricted to models that read both the baseline profile and the dynamic streams (dyn-prof-v2) by protocol gt-primary-2026-10-13-fusion, declared on 2026-10-13 after the test segment had already been inspected in gt-primary-2026-10-11-v1 and gt-primary-2026-10-11-v2. The validation score, tie margin and tie-break are unchanged.
* On people held out entirely (gt-nested-2026-10-12-v1), adding the profile to LightGBM changed mean absolute error by +0.34 [-0.28, +0.89] mg/dL at 30 min; -0.75 [-2.19, +0.61] mg/dL at 60 min (negative = better; participant-cluster bootstrap). Every interval includes zero: no reliable benefit was shown.
* Profile values are constant within a person and the model is trained and tested on the same 14 people, so the profile can act as a participant identifier. A better score here does not show that the profile helps unseen patients.
* This model was selected to implement the required fusion of static and dynamic data, not because it was more accurate. We have not established that profile features improve forecasting for unseen patients.
* The warning margin (-10 mg/dL; gt-primary-2026-10-11-v2: -30) was re-derived by the same event-level F1 rule, and margins -10, +0 were within 0.03 F1 of the best on validation. Because the model and its margin changed together, the change in false alarms per observed day (2.95 to 1.49) and in detected onsets (64 to 61 of 64) is not evidence that the profile improves warnings.

## Limitations

* Small cohort: 14 participants with Type 2 diabetes (CGMacros, US, recruited 2021-2024); overlapping forecast windows are not independent patients.
* No Indian participants: nothing here establishes performance for patients in India.
* The study CGMs were blinded; real-time availability is a replay approximation (zero transmission latency).
* Native Dexcom readings were recovered from a 1-minute interpolated table; timestamps are known only to the minute.
* Medication, insulin use and illness were not recorded and cannot be modelled.
* Meal logs are excluded from the primary model because their real-time availability is undocumented.
* Prediction intervals are split-conformal under time-series dependence; observed test coverage is reported and no coverage guarantee is claimed.
* Warning scores are uncalibrated forecast margins in mg/dL, not probabilities.
* Research event definition (>180 mg/dL sustained 15 minutes) is not a clinical triage threshold.
* Retrospective research replay; no clinician, clinical workflow or patient outcome was studied.
* Corrected retrospective result of `gt-primary-2026-10-11-v1`: same test segment, already inspected.
